# 🚀 STEMM

> Stop drowning in long research papers and unorganized lecture slides. Just upload your PDF, and let **STEMM** cook.

**STEMM** is an AI-powered study platform designed to remove the friction, complex prompting, and prompt engineering bottlenecks of standard LLMs. Built specifically for modern university students (Science, Technology, Engineering, Math, and Management), **STEMM** turns raw PDFs into personalized, interactive learning hubs instantly.

[🌐 Live Demo](https://stemm.vercel.app) · [💬 Report a Bug](https://github.com) · [💡 Request Feature](https://github.com)

---

## ⚡ The Problem vs. The STEMM Way

- **The Old Way**: Copy-pasting 50 pages of text into ChatGPT, running out of context windows, crafting 20-line prompts, and trying to format the messy output into study notes.
- **The STEMM Way**: Drag, drop, and let it cook. One upload gives you contextual flashcards, complex formula breakdown, and case study solvers tailored to your specific major.

---

## ✨ Features Crafted for Your Major

### 🔬 For Science & Tech Students

- **Formula Breakdown**: Instantly deciphers complex mathematical equations and physics proofs with step-by-step logic.
- **Code Generator & Explainer**: Upload raw technical documentation to auto-generate clean code samples and logic flowcharts.

### 📊 For Management & Finance Students

- **Case Study Analyzer**: Summarizes lengthy business case studies into core problem statements, SWAT matrices, and strategic takeaways.
- **Data Synthesis**: Extracts embedded financial tables and converts them into actionable, readable breakdowns.

### 🛠️ Core All-in-One Engine

- **Semantic PDF Chat**: High-precision Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) ensures zero hallucinations and source-backed answers.
- **Instant Quiz Gen**: Converts any chapter into flashcards and mock exams with a single click.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: Next.js 15 (App Router), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
- **UI Components**: shadcn/ui, Radix UI, Lucide React
- **AI Engine**: LangChain / Vercel AI SDK
- **Database & Auth**: Prisma ORM, PostgreSQL, NextAuth.js / Clerk
